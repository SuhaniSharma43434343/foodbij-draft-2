# About Foodbiz - what you need to know

- **Who:** Foodbiz, a catering and hospitality company in Mumbai (the company behind it is Foodies Hospitality).
  Founders: Amar Jog and Kushal (Kush) Shetty. You will not deal with them directly.
- **What they do:** catering for weddings, corporate offices, brand launches, live events and concerts, and
  children's parties. Menus range from regional Indian and traditional to global, contemporary and fusion, plus live
  food counters ("live stations").
- **How they see themselves:** "We are not just in the business of food, we are in the business of food and
  experience." Service first. Hassle-free: "outsource all the stress to us, be present and enjoy." Honest and
  transparent. Most new clients come from old clients' recommendations.
- **Lines they already use:** "Where excellence meets elegance." "This is how we groove it." The new logo tagline:
  "Service - Innovation - Trust".
- **Who they have worked for** (from their presentations and the photo folders in `05-assets/images/`):
  Ferrari, Mercedes-Benz, Land Rover, Hublot, Cartier, Netflix, Crunchyroll, Bloomberg, SBI, UTI, Motilal Oswal,
  Raymond, Air India Express, Red Bull, Rolling Loud, among others. **Naming a client on the site needs the client's
  OK; we do not have it yet.** Fine on review builds, flag it in your NOTES.md.
- **Sub-brands (later, not in v1):** Moira (weddings), Caterpillar (daily corporate dining), Gummy Gourmet
  (children's catering), The Travelling Circus (gourmet sit-down dinners). Pillar Live has been dropped.
- **What they want the site to feel like:** opulence, luxury, lifestyle; contemporary, stylish, simple. Their 2025
  wish list also said: international and modern ("like out of Denmark"), no chef hats or crossed-cutlery cliches.
