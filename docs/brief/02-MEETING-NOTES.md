# What the client said - website review, 2026-09-30

On the call: Purusharth (Kami Labs), Amar Jog (Foodbiz founder) and Sanaya Dastoor (Foodbiz brand and marketing).
Quotes are from the call recording, lightly cleaned up.

---

## 1. Decisions

1. **Lead the site with services, not sub-brands.** Amar: "a brilliant suggestion... it will clear the clutter."
   Sub-brand names and logos stay off this version.
2. **Sub-brand micro-sites come later**, each its own experience.
3. **The main Foodbiz site comes first**, as simple as the reference site (obcatering.com). Homepage and service
   pages matter most.
4. **AI-generated photos and video loops are fine for now**; a professional shoot comes later.
5. **Use the new logo.** Sanaya: the old site's background was "dull... very shaadi"; the new colours should feel
   contemporary and modern.
6. **Speed matters.** Their season starts in November and they want to start marketing the site.

## 2. What they said about our old site

**Liked**
- On a TV or wide screen it "looks very grand".
- The opening showing a plate being laid out: it says "these guys are a catering company and they dwell in luxury
  catering".
- Live food stations being shown, but only as a teaser, not full menus.

**Did not like**
- **Mobile typography:** "They are not at all lifestyle. They are not at all luxury. They are not at all refined."
- **The landing page:** "this could be a data company or a solutions company or analytics. It does not give you the
  feel of a luxury lifestyle catering company."
- **Navigation:** "the content was fully loaded, fully packed, but the navigation required a fair amount of skill" to
  discover things. Do not make a visitor who is not good with technology hunt.
- **Too much:** "our site is offering too much... this is not our pride wall."

## 3. What they want

- **Keywords:** opulence, luxury, lifestyle. Cool, contemporary, very stylish.
- **"The greatest of the stories are the ones which are told simply."** Content makes it special.
- **Food, service, presentation** on every page. "We are going to be a food company."
- **The reference site** (https://www.obcatering.com): clean navigation, a simple story, the business split into
  occasions, and a section of "gorgeous looking food". Amar loved it on a wide screen but said on a phone it is weak.
  So: copy the structure and the calm, not their mobile site.
- **Short event stories that start from the brief.** Not who the photographer was; what the client asked for, then
  what Foodbiz made. Examples Amar gave (we still need the facts from the client):
  - a wedding for a new-age couple who did not want a typical wedding: the outdoors, a food-loving family;
  - a wedding for a business family with overseas guests: Indian specialities with a modern twist;
  - a Ferrari Purosangue launch, a Crunchyroll show, and an upcoming Maserati launch.
- **About five top events per category**, added over time.
- **Real photos as a strength (optional):** almost all their photos are phone-shot at real events. Amar suggested
  "real events, real photos, nothing staged" could be a message. He is fine if we do not use it.

## 4. How Amar describes the business

Three areas: (1) office and corporate catering, (2) brand, luxury and lifestyle, (3) concerts and large formats.
One common language: food.

The future sub-brands and the look he imagines for each (for later micro-sites; useful as mood for the matching
service now):

| Area | Future sub-brand | Look he described | v1 service |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weddings and social functions | Moira | golds, bronzes, rich colours | 1 Weddings / 2 Celebrations |
| Children's parties | Gummy Gourmet | "you have just entered Disneyland" | 2 Celebrations & private dining |
| Gourmet sit-down dinners | The Travelling Circus | experimental, eclectic, "a little madness" | 2 Celebrations & private dining |
| Daily catering for offices, towers, IT parks | Caterpillar | (not described) | 4 Corporate & office dining |
| Corporate, lifestyle and luxury events | Foodbiz itself | contemporary, stark, modern, minimal | 3 Brand launches & live events |

- **Pillar Live is dropped:** "We don't have Pillar Live, we are reducing the numbers." Large events remain a service.
- **Venues and banquets** belong with weddings later. No Venues page now.

## 5. Not in this version

Sub-brand micro-sites, any AI chat or brochure tools, and the professional photo shoot. All later.
